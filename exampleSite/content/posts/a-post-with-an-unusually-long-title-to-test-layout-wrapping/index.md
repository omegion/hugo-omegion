## Introduction

This post exists mainly to exercise a long title. Real posts usually settle
on something short and punchy, but titles that run long still happen — and
the theme needs to hold up when one does.

## What this checks

A long title can misbehave in a few places at once:

- The post list, where the title sits next to a date and possibly tags
- The sidebar, if recent posts are surfaced there
- The article header, where the title is usually the largest text on the page
- The `<title>` tag and any social meta tags used for link previews

## Conclusion

If the layout still reads cleanly with a title this long, shorter titles
should never be a problem.
